Who we target, and the weekly routine

A good account

  • 200 or more chairs at one site, or several sites in one city
  • A facilities, admin or workplace team that owns furniture upkeep
  • Likely to sign a yearly maintenance contract, not a one-off repair
  • Looks like who we already serve: Wipro, CBRE, Colliers, Embassy, Indiqube

Where to look first

  • Facility management firms: one contract opens many client sites
  • Coworking operators: thousands of shared chairs, heavy wear
  • Office parks and landlords: tenant services across campuses
  • Large IT campuses, hospitals and universities
  • Tenders, every Monday: GeM, CPPP, Karnataka. Search "furniture repair". The scout adds tenders it sees in the news.

Weekly routine

  1. Monday: research 10 accounts. Find the facilities or admin head through LinkedIn, the company site, or a referral from a current customer.
  2. Tuesday to Thursday: reach out. Call, email and LinkedIn, three touches over ten days.
  3. Friday: review. Move anyone who replied to the pipeline, mark poor fits, plan next week.

Aim for 20 to 40 strong accounts a quarter, not hundreds of small ones.
